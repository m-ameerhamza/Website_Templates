Who We Are

Where Austin
Unwinds

Tucked into 200 W 6th St of Midtown, Sky Lounge is Austin's favourite late-night rooftop hangout. With a cozy, library-esque indoor setting and a breezy rooftop that offers stunning sunset views over the city, it's the kind of place you come to for dinner and stay until 2 AM.

"Beautiful rooftop with amazing views... very lively and aesthetic vibe. Perfect for relaxing and spending quality time." — Kayla M., Local Guide
⏰ Open 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM · Hours may vary on public holidays

Popular with young professionals, couples, and friend groups looking for a laid-back evening — the ambiance alone is worth the visit. Whether you're on the rooftop enjoying the breeze or settled inside, Sky Lounge always delivers the vibe.

What Makes Us Different

The Sky Lounge
Experience

It's not just a café — it's a state of mind. Here's what keeps Austin coming back night after night.

🌇
Rooftop Views
Watch Austin's skyline glow as the sun sets. Our rooftop offers picturesque panoramic views — the best seat in Midtown.
💨
Cocktail Lounge
Our premium cocktail selection is the talk of Austin — featured in 31 reviews. Wide range of craft mixes in a relaxed, open-air rooftop setting.
🌙
Open Late
When the rest of the city is asleep, Sky Lounge is just getting started. Open until 2:30 AM — the perfect spot for night owls.
📚
Library Aesthetic
Cozy indoor seating with a library-esque atmosphere. Warm lighting, thoughtful décor, and a mood that invites long conversations.
🍽️
Comfort Menu
From BBQ Smoked Chicken to creamy pastas and fresh sandwiches — light comfort food done right, paired with mocktails and specialty coffee.
🌧️
Rain-Ready
A covered shed ensures the vibe never stops — even on rainy evenings. Families love the option to move indoors without losing the atmosphere.
