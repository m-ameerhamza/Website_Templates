Our Story

Real Authentic
Taste of China

Tucked into a charming spot on Harbor Lane, River District, Golden Wok is not a restaurant you stumble upon — it's one you seek out. Founded by a passionate family of culinary artists, it has quietly become Portland's most beloved destination for authentic Chinese cuisine.

"After years of searching, I finally found real authentic Chinese food here in Portland. The flavors transported me back to my time studying in Shanghai." — Sophie L., Local Guide
